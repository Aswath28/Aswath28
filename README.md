### About me

I'm a Product Manager at **ServiceNow** in Bengaluru, leading the **conversational AI Agents** platform — a 0→1 product that compresses enterprise ITSM setup from 240+ days to under 30. Before PM, I was an engineer at **Robert Bosch** building EV navigation systems for OEMs across the globe. MBA from **IIM Trichy**. ~6 years in product, mostly at the messy intersection of AI, platforms, and enterprise software.

These days I'm building in public — using Claude as a thought partner to ship side projects, sharpen my AI PM craft, and document the journey along the way.
